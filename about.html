Our Story

Good food, friendly faces and a whole lot of nostalgia — that's what has kept Ephraim coming back to The Malt Shop on Main Street, year after year.

Once upon a malt

An Ephraim Original

On the corner of Main Street in Ephraim, Utah, there's a little diner that has quietly become part of the town's story. A longstanding staple in Sanpete County, The Malt Shop is where locals grab a booth after ballgames, where Snow College students fuel up between classes, and where road-trippers pull over because a hand-spun malt is always worth the stop.

Inside, it's pure classic Americana: Coca-Cola memorabilia lining the walls, an old-time diner feel, and the steady hum of the fryer and the milkshake mixer. The kitchen keeps it simple and does it right — burgers, sandwiches, fries, shakes, malts, sundaes and salads, made to order and served with a smile. The options are endless!

A Little Time Machine on Main Street

Walk in and you'll get it right away: Coca-Cola memorabilia on the walls, a cozy old-time diner vibe, and the smell of the grill doing the rest. It's the kind of place where the staff remembers your order and nobody rushes your milkshake.

Folks from Ephraim, Snow College and every road trip through Sanpete County pull up a chair — some for the food, some for the nostalgia, most for both.

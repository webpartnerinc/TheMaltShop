Before You Stop By

A few handy notes for first-timers: takeout is available if you're dashing between Snow College classes or heading back on the road, booths fill up fast around mealtimes, and Sundays we're closed — the crew is off enjoying their own family dinners.

And if it's your first visit, do yourself a favor: order a malt. Reviewers don't call them heavenly for nothing.
